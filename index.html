About the Game

A therapeutic puzzle journey around the globe.

Travel Decor: Journey Match invites players to accompany a traveling protagonist through peaceful villages and vibrant cities, solving relaxing match-3 puzzles along the way. Each completed level rewards travel-inspired items used to decorate cozy personal rest stops around the world. The tone should feel gentle, immersive, and mindful, appealing to players seeking a calm, low-pressure puzzle escape.

How to play

Simple matches, worldwide discovery.

  • Explore New Regions

    Progress unlocks distinct villages and cities worldwide.

  • Match Travel-Themed Tiles

    Swap and match three or more elements to clear levels.

  • Collect Travel Memories

    Earn decorative items as level rewards.

  • Decorate Rest Stops

    Personalize cozy spaces with collected travel items.

  • Use Smart Boosters

    Apply travel-themed power-ups to clear tricky boards.
